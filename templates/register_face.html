Best Practices for Registration
  • Lighting: Ensure even, front-facing room lighting. Avoid bright windows directly behind the student.
  • Head Angles: Look directly at the camera, then turn slightly left, right, up, and down during the 25-frame capture.
  • Expressions: Keep a neutral expression for some frames and a slight smile for others to give LBPH diverse features.
  • Occlusion: Keep hair, hands, and hats away from eyes, nose, and mouth. Glasses are fine if normally worn.
